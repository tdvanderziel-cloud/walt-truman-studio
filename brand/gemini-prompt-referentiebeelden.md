# Gemini-prompts: referentiebeelden van Walt

Upload bij elke prompt `character-sheet.jpg` mee. Maak de twee beelden apart, één per opdracht, en sla ze op als `walt-reference-front.png` en `walt-reference-34.png` in deze map.

## 1. Vooraanzicht

```
Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference for this puppet character, Walt Truman. Keep his face, wavy dark hair with gray temples, thick eyebrows, copper half-moon reading glasses low on his nose, terracotta felt skin, navy blazer, white open-collar shirt and round checkmark lapel pin completely identical.

Create one single high-resolution photo of him, front view, framed from the mid-chest up, as a news anchor looking straight into the camera with his calm, warm, slightly crooked smile. Mouth closed. Shoulders relaxed, head very slightly tilted.

Background: a perfectly flat, even chroma key green (#00FF00), no shadows, no gradient, no floor, no props. Soft, even studio lighting on the puppet, with no green light spilling onto him. No visible rods, no hands in frame, no text, no labels. Photorealistic handmade felt puppet, visible felt fibers and stitching. Vertical image, 1024 x 1536.
```

## 2. Driekwartaanzicht

```
Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference for this puppet character, Walt Truman. Keep everything identical: face, hair, eyebrows, copper half-moon reading glasses, terracotta felt skin, navy blazer, white shirt and lapel pin.

Create one single high-resolution photo of him in a three-quarter view, turned slightly to the right, framed from the mid-chest up, looking at the camera as if he is about to tell you something interesting. Mouth closed, one eyebrow very slightly raised.

Background: a perfectly flat, even chroma key green (#00FF00), no shadows, no gradient, no props. Soft, even studio lighting, no green spill on the puppet. No visible rods, no hands in frame, no text. Photorealistic handmade felt puppet. Vertical image, 1024 x 1536.
```

## Optioneel: de vaste gebaren

Met hetzelfde begin ("Use the attached character sheet as the exact reference...") en dezelfde achtergrondregels, één beeld per gebaar:

- looking over the top of his glasses, one eyebrow raised high, mouth pulled to one side (skeptical)
- eyebrows high, mouth wide open (surprised)
- nodding, slight smile (agreeing)
- holding a rubber stamp up, about to stamp (verdict)

## Controle

Leg elk resultaat naast het karakterblad en check: bril (koper, halve maan, laag op de neus), haar (grijze slapen), speldje, blazer, en geen groen op de pop zelf. Klopt iets niet, vraag dan alleen dat ene detail te repareren.
